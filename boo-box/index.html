Questions

Good to know

Can I pick a different mix of cupcakes?
Not for the Boo Box. Every box is the same six characters, which is what makes a one-hour neighborhood delivery possible. For a custom mix or a bigger order, reach out and we'll set it up as a custom order.
Do I need to be home?
No. We'll knock between 7:00 and 8:00 PM. If no one answers, the box goes by your door and we take a photo at drop-off. Late October evenings are cool enough that the buttercream is perfectly happy on the porch for a bit.
I live just outside Harpers Mill. Can I order?
This one is for Harpers Mill addresses only, so delivery stays a single loop on a school night. If you're nearby and want Halloween cupcakes, send us a note and we'll see what we can do as a custom order.
When should we eat them?
They're best within two days of delivery, so Wednesday night through Friday. Keep the box at room temperature, out of the sun. Think of it as a week-of-Halloween treat rather than one to save for Saturday night.
What about allergies?
The Boo Box contains wheat, milk, egg, and soy. Everything is made in a home kitchen that also handles peanuts and tree nuts, so we can't guarantee anything is free of cross-contact. Each box carries a full ingredient and allergen label.
Can I order more than one box?
Yes. Add the Boo Box to your cart and change the quantity. Every box in the order goes to the same address.
What if it sells out?
There are 30 boxes and no second delivery night. When they're gone, the button on this page switches to Sold Out. You can still reach out about a custom order.
