Questions

Good to know

Do I stay, or can I drop off?
Either. Parents are welcome to stay and watch, and plenty will. If you'd rather drop off, that's fine too: pick-up is 1:00 PM at the clubhouse. You choose at checkout, and we ask for a cell number either way.
What about allergies?
We won't bring peanut butter, nut candies, or nut toppings. The cupcakes and cake pops contain wheat, milk, and egg, and some toppings contain soy. Everything is made in a home kitchen that also handles peanuts and tree nuts, so we can't guarantee anything is free of cross-contact. Please list any food allergies at checkout, and every box that goes home carries a full ingredient and allergen label.
What should my kid wear?
Something that can meet frosting. They'll decorate and wear an apron, but sprinkles have a way of traveling.
I have two kids who want to come.
Wonderful. Reserve one ticket per child so each one has their own name, age, and allergy details on file. Add the first ticket to your cart, then come back and add the second.
Is this only for Harpers Mill residents?
No. It's hosted at the Harpers Mill Clubhouse, and anyone is welcome to reserve a spot.
My kid is 7, or 13.
This first workshop is built for ages 8 to 12. Join the waitlist and tell us the age; if there's enough interest we'll plan a session for them.
What if it sells out?
Ten spots will go quickly. When they're gone the button on this page switches to a waitlist, and the waitlist hears about the next workshop before anyone else.
