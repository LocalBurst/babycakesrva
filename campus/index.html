Questions

Good to know

Where exactly is the hand-off?
A central, easy-to-reach spot in Blacksburg, close to campus. Hand-off runs 3:00–5:00 PM on Friday, September 4 — we text the exact location to your student (and email you) once orders close.
What if my student can't make the window?
Have them text us — we'll do our best to work something out during our Friday delivery run. Boxes that can't be delivered aren't refundable once baked, so a quick heads-up goes a long way.
What about allergens?
Our cupcakes contain wheat, milk, eggs, and soy, and are made in a home kitchen that also processes peanuts and tree nuts. Every box is individually labeled with full ingredient and allergen information.
Will there be more drops after this one?
That's the plan — this is our first Blacksburg drop, and if it sells out we'll be back on a regular schedule this fall. Follow @babycakesrva to hear about the next one first.
Can I order for myself instead of a student?
Absolutely — parents in town for the weekend, locals, students treating themselves. Anyone can order; the hand-off works the same way.
